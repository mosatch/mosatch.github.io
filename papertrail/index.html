Papertrail

Batch scanning for the ScanSnap S1500M, with searchable PDFs at the end of it.

Download Papertrail 0.2

12.5 MB · Apple silicon · macOS 26 or later

Installing

  1. Open the disk image and drag Papertrail to your Applications folder.
  2. Plug the scanner in and switch it on.
  3. Open Papertrail and pick a folder for the library. That folder holds nothing but PDFs, so it stays useful without the app.

Nothing else to install — the scanner driver ships inside the app. Later versions arrive on their own; Papertrail checks for them once a day.

Scanning

Press the Scan button on the front of the scanner and feed the stack. Each page is read with OCR, turned the right way up, and filed as a PDF you can search in Spotlight.

Papertrail bundles SANE, which is free software under the GPL. The corresponding source is at the link above, and the full notice ships with the app and is also here.
